# Recipe — Izzie Doodle (DRAFT, for Jon/Jennifer approval)

Sources: Jennifer's draft description ("a petite siamese/snowbengal mix") + the photo of her real cat, received 2026-11-11. Draft only — nothing here is canon until Jon/Jennifer approve it.

## Look
- Petite Siamese / snow-Bengal mix cat.
- Pale lynx-point coat: cream, soft gray, and warm tan; points slightly darker on face, ears, legs, tail.
- Vivid blue eyes — her most striking feature.
- Tabby striping on forehead, face, and legs; cream chest and belly.
- Tail darkens toward the tip, ending in black rings.
- Small pink-brown nose; neat, compact build.

## NEVER CHANGE
- Blue eyes.
- Lynx-point markings (striped face/legs, pale body).
- Black-ringed tail tip.
- Petite — she reads smaller/younger next to Frittee, her big sister.

## Feel
- Sweet, friendly, earnest — the baker everybody in town loves.
- A cheerful workaholic who insists she's fine and forgets what day it is.
- Expressive ears and tail when she's flustered or touched.

## Signature bits
- Bakery apron at work (in the draft she even falls asleep wearing it).
- Kneading — the book's title gag: cat "biscuits" are what Izzie makes, in every sense.
- Her shop: **Izzie's Biscuits** — biscuits, pies, and snacks in a cozy bakery.
