## The story in one paragraph
Izzie Doodle runs a beloved bakery and works so hard she forgets her own birthday. When her sister Frittee ropes her into a "top secret" baking mission and she runs out of ingredients, Izzie collects them from the friends who love her bakery — and discovers the mission was her surprise birthday party all along, with everyone baking together. Sweet, funny, and very punny; the heart of it is learning to rest and let your community help you.
