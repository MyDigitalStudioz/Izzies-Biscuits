Final Pass — Script Stage — Izzie’s Biscuits

Every page of Jennifer’s draft, carded in order — the same board used for the Final Pass on Harlow’s Accidental Journey. There is no art yet: each card carries the scene exactly as the script describes it, with its status underneath. When production starts, renders land on these cards, one page at a time, and this board becomes the review page.

P01
Izzie kneading dough in her kitchen — store sign: Izzie’s Biscuits
P01 · SCRIPT — The bakery
This is a story about Izzie Doodle, a sweet and friendly cat who owns a bakery, famous for biscuits — and yummy pies.
Script

P02 · SCRIPT — Everyone delights
Everyone delights in Izzie’s delicious snacks — they come very often, and Izzie adores getting to know everyone that way.
Script

Izzie loves baking — but she works so much she never takes any time off, and sometimes forgets what day it is.
Script

Somepurrdy unexpected: Izzie’s sister Frittee, in person, with something very important to ask — food for a big party, tonight.

Izzie loves her sister and always wants to make her proud. A president? A prime minister? Who could the mission be for? She sets off baking.

Her snacks were so popular this morning that she’s out of all the ingredients — no sugar, no salt, no flour. She decides to ask everyone for help.
